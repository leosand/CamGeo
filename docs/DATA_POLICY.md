# Open Data Policy & Ethics Charter

CamGeo operates as a digital public good dedicated to open environmental science, education, and regional analytical capacity.

## 1. Licensing Framework

- **Source Code**: Apache License 2.0. Permissive open-source licensing ensuring unencumbered community contribution and enhancement.
- **Data Products & Benchmarks**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0). Users are free to share, adapt, and build upon the data for research, civil society, governance, and application development, provided appropriate provenance credit is maintained.

## 2. Ethical Safeguards & Sensitive Spatial Data

Earth observation data can intersect with delicate territorial, environmental, and social contexts. CamGeo strictly implements responsible spatial data stewardship:

1. **Protection of Vulnerable Sites**: Precise spatial coordinates of endangered wildlife nesting zones, sensitive indigenous settlements, or cultural heritage sites are deliberately obfuscated or aggregated to coarse grid boundaries.
2. **Non-Attribution of Land Tenure**: Classification products reflect physical land cover and vegetative dynamics; they do not constitute legal surveys, cadastral titling, or ownership demarcation.
3. **Impartial Provenance**: Every raster asset and vector training package is accompanied by complete algorithmic manifests detailing input source registries, timestamps, and model evaluation boundaries.
