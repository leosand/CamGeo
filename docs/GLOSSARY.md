# CamGeo Glossary

Every acronym and technical term used in CamGeo documents, defined in clear language.

## A

- **AI (Artificial Intelligence)** — computational systems learning predictive patterns from data.
- **AOI (Area Of Interest)** — the geographic boundary enclosing a spatial study or processing task.
- **ATBD (Algorithm Theoretical Basis Document)** — formal documentation detailing the scientific formulations behind a remote-sensing product.
- **Agroforestry (Shaded Tree Crops)** — agricultural systems combining an upper tree canopy with understory perennial crops (e.g., cocoa, coffee).

## B

- **Backscatter (SAR)** — the portion of a radar signal reflected back to the satellite antenna, sensitive to vegetation structure and moisture.
- **Biome** — a major regional ecological community characterized by distinctive vegetation and climate regimes.

## C

- **CC-BY-4.0** — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.
- **COG (Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF)** — a GeoTIFF formatted to allow streaming and reading of specific sub-windows via HTTP range requests.
- **Cross-Ratio (Radar)** — ratio of cross-polarized to co-polarized radar backscatter (VH/VV), indicative of volume scattering in forest canopies.

## D

- **DEM (Digital Elevation Model)** — a continuous raster representing topographic surface elevation.
- **DOI (Digital Object Identifier)** — a persistent alphanumeric string providing a citable link to published research data.
- **Dual-Polarization (SAR)** — transmitting and receiving radar pulses in different orthogonal wave orientations (e.g., VV, VH).

## F

- **F1 Score** — harmonic mean of precision and recall for a classification category.
- **FLAIR-HUB** — benchmark dataset and multi-sensor land cover challenge initiated by the French National Institute of Geographic and Forest Information (IGN).

## G

- **GEE (Google Earth Engine)** — cloud platform for planetary-scale geospatial analysis.
- **GeoParquet** — columnar vector data storage format optimized for cloud querying.

## L

- **LULC (Land Use and Land Cover)** — classification describing physical surface materials and human utilization of land.

## M

- **MapBiomas** — multi-institutional collaborative network producing annual land cover and land use maps using automated classification.
- **MNDWI / NDWI** — Normalized Difference Water Indices, capturing open moisture and surface water bodies.

## N

- **NDBI** — Normalized Difference Built-up Index, highlighting impervious urban structures.
- **NDVI** — Normalized Difference Vegetation Index, quantifying canopy greenness and photosynthetically active biomass.

## O

- **Olofsson Protocol** — widely accepted statistical methodology for estimating area and assessing accuracy of land change maps with confidence intervals.

## R

- **Random Forest (RF)** — ensemble machine learning classifier combining multiple decision trees.

## S

- **SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar)** — active microwave sensor capable of imaging Earth's surface through clouds and at night.
- **SCL (Scene Classification Layer)** — pixel-quality classification mask provided with Sentinel-2 L2A data.
- **STAC (SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog)** — standardized specification for describing and discovering spatial-temporal assets.
