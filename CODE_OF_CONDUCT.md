# Code of Conduct

CamGeo follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1. This is a short, plain-language version.

## Our commitment

We want a community where everyone feels welcome and safe, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Welcome newcomers and different skill levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Respect local contexts: many contributors work on sensitive environmental and community topics in Cameroon.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks — in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual language or imagery in community spaces.
- Bad-faith arguments, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

- Report problems by opening a confidential issue or by contacting the Steering Committee chair (contact listed in the repository when the committee is formed).
- Reports are reviewed quickly and confidentially.
- Consequences range from a private warning to removal from the project, depending on severity and repetition.
- Decisions about conduct belong to the Steering Committee and are not subject to public vote.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, calls, chat channels, and when someone represents CamGeo in public.

---

*If you are unsure whether something is acceptable, ask. Asking is always safe.*
